Automated 90-Day Medical Cooldown Policy

To adhere to global medical safety guidelines, donors are automatically placed on a 90-day cooldown after any donation. During this time, they do not appear in emergency search results and cannot donate again until their full rest period is reached.

How BloodLink Works

A simple, transparent four-step workflow designed to save lives during critical medical emergencies.

1

Register

Volunteers register with their blood group, location, and past donation history through our quick verification form.

2

Search

Patients or hospitals filter instantly by blood group (e.g. O+, AB-) and city to locate eligible donors.

3

Connect

Directly contact available donors with clear contact information without running into ineligible candidates.

4

Save Lives

Record donations to update the donor's medical timeline and initiate their automated 90-day cooldown cycle.

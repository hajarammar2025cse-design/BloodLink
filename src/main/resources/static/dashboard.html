90-Day Medical Rule Enforcement

Clinical Safety

The system continuously evaluates donor eligibility. When a donation is logged, the donor automatically enters a mandatory 90-day cooldown period to restore red blood cell levels safely.

Cooldown Calculation Rule:
eligibleDate = lastDonationDate + 90 days
isAvailable = (today >= eligibleDate)
  • Ineligible donors are hidden from search results.
  • Automatic re-enabling when 90 days elapse.
  • Attempts to log another donation are rejected with 400 Bad Request.
